# Review of 2026-09-08 : decisions folded into the remaining waves

A critical review of the build (optimisation mechanics, data sources, backtesting, viability) after the
engine wave, and the decisions the user accepted. Each decision names where it lands: a contract
section (through amendment C1c), a package, a wave. `docs/PLAN_V3_WAVES.md` "Part 3" carries the
resulting sequence. Numbers quoted come from `data/datasets/y2026` (287 markets: 220 Kalshi, 67
Manifold; folds 111 train, 46 validation, 130 sealed; 53 illiquid; 792 of 7 391 Wikipedia items linked).

## A. Defects to fix before anything else builds on them

| # | Finding | Decision | Lands in |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-R1 | Folds by calendar thirds put most Kalshi markets in the last two months: 111 train, 46 validation, 130 sealed. The pyramid is inverted. | Folds are cut by **count quantiles** of resolution order, 60 / 20 / 20 by default, still chronological. The manifest reports the cut dates and counts. | Contract 7.7 and 12.7 (C1c ruling); builder (D6 fix in package DS1); rebuild of `y2026`. |
| D-R2 | Two markets of one event can fall in different folds; a train price path encodes a sealed outcome and the cross-asset sensor or a cluster would carry it. | **Cluster-aware folds**: every market of an `EventCluster` (and of a Kalshi event ticker) is assigned to the fold of its latest resolution. The tester and the claims refuse a dataset whose folds split a cluster. | Contract 7.7, 16.3 (C1c); builder (DS1); `pmx data verify` gains the check. |
| D-R3 | The six-hour safety lag on Wikipedia daily pages is a guess. | Each bullet is stamped with the **revision time at which it first appeared** on the day page (revision history of `Portal:Current events/<day>`); the blanket lag remains only as a floor. | Contract 7.3 (C1c); D5 fix in DS1; rebuild. |
| D-R4 | The linker's precision is unmeasured. | A **manual precision audit** of 50 random links per build, stored as `audit/linker_<hash>.json` with the reviewer's verdicts; the manifest reports precision, and a build below 80 percent precision is labelled `news_links: "weak"` in every leaderboard using news sensors. | DS1 (tooling), gate G3 (the review itself). |
| D-R5 | The daily grid kills Kalshi's intraday content (median life 29 days, 1-minute candlesticks published) and the latency rule costs a full day. | **Hourly grid becomes the headline Kalshi grid** (`y2026h`); daily stays for Manifold and for the demo pack; minute grids for the event studies. | DS1 (build), contract 5.2 note (C1c). |
| D-R6 | The contamination audit detects blatant recall only. | Label it as such: LLM leaderboards carry `contamination_audit: "coarse"`, and an LLM result never enters a claim before the live book replicates it. | Contract 11.5, 12.8 (C1c); A6, O4. |

## B. Statistical power and optimisation mechanics

| # | Finding | Decision | Lands in |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-R7 | 111 training markets cannot detect a Brier edge of 0.005 (market-level SD about 0.15); 48 x 30 evaluations on them make the deflated bound unreachable. | The evolutionary loop is declared a **generator**, not a certifier; certification comes from the sealed fold of datasets of thousands of markets and from the live book. Targets: 2 000 Kalshi and 2 000 Manifold markets at the hourly grid; the per-provider cap is lifted (kept only as a debug option). | PRD v3 3.1 stays; DS1 (universe rule and build); O1 docs. |
| D-R8 | Bonferroni over the rule miner's millions of candidates kills everything or invites cheating. | **Pre-registered hypothesis families** per generation (a family is a template such as "category x horizon x price band"), **Benjamini-Hochberg** false discovery rate control within a family at q = 0.05, and **out-of-time replication** (validation fold, then live record) as the primary promotion criterion. The candidate count of every family is journaled. | Contract 12.6 and 18 (C1c); S2. |
| D-R9 | Full engine replay per genome is impossible on hourly and minute grids for a whole population. | **Two-tier fitness**: a proxy fitness on precomputed integer feature matrices (bar-level price-move value and directional Brier of the belief function) for exploration; the full engine replay for the elites, the archive cells and every claim. Both tiers journal what they used; a genome never enters a claim on proxy fitness. | Contract 12.6 and 12.11 (C1c); O2; the `learn/env` precomputation of rung 3 is pulled forward as `pmx/features/matrix.py` (wave 6). |
| D-R10 | Kalshi fees (7 percent of p(1-p)) and thin books make most micro-edges untradeable; PnL failure will be liquidity, not skill. | Forecast skill and money stay separate (already), and a **capacity metric** is added: the dollar size at which the volume cap and impact halve the strategy's after-fee return, per market and aggregated. Claims report capacity beside PnL. | Contract 12.3 (C1c); E5/O4 (wave 6). |
| D-R11 | Prompt mutation for LLM agents has about two and a half months of clean markets to learn from. | O3 is **deferred** until the live book holds at least 100 resolved markets; the plan keeps the package text. | Plan part 3. |
| D-R12 | Manifold is play money. | Unchanged rule, restated as a gate check: no leaderboard, chart or README sentence pools Manifold with Kalshi; every Manifold number carries `currency: mana`. | Gates G3 onward. |
| D-R13 | The Kalshi universe (731 series) was picked ad hoc and is category-biased. | A **documented universe rule** in the manifest: every Kalshi series with at least N settled markets in the window and at least V dollars of volume, listed by the builder from the settled walk, with the counts before and after; the series list becomes an output, not an input. Weather series are kept but tagged `forecastable_from_public_models`. | DS1; contract 7.4 (C1c). |

## D. What the review confirmed and leaves untouched

As-of enforced by the engine and tested by poisoned-future injections; integers everywhere; replay by
hash; the sealed fold with a claims ledger and a computed candidate count; the separation of forecast
skill from trading result; negative results as first-class results; the adversarial market maker bounded
by the tape. The risk of the product is not self-deception, the design forbids it; it is spending
optimisation effort where the data cannot prove anything, which sections A to C address.
